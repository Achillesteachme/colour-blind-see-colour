Colour Blind: See Colour

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Colour Blind: See Colour ("the App") is built to help people with colour vision deficiency see the world more clearly. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the App and improve your experience. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Data We Collect

Account & Authentication

If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address to send you a magic sign-in link. No password is stored. Authentication is handled securely by Supabase.

Photos

Photos you capture or import are stored locally on your device. If you are signed in, photos may be synced to secure cloud storage (Supabase) so you can access them across devices. Photos are never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Camera & Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your camera and photo library solely to apply colour correction filters. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted without your consent.

Subscription & Purchase Data

Subscription billing is handled by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. We do not store your payment card details.

Usage Data

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as which colour correction type is most used) to improve the App. This data cannot be used to identify you.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the colour correction features of the App
  • To sync your saved photos across your devices (if signed in)
  • To manage your subscription and restore purchases
  • To send you your sign-in magic link (if you request one)
  • To respond to support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We work with the following trusted services solely to operate the App:

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud photo storage
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Apple App Store — payment processing

Each of these services has their own privacy policy and complies with applicable data protection laws.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your photos at any time from within the App. To request full deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us at the email below and we will action your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to authorised services only. No security system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
